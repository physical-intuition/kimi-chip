# X1-Y4 Design Notes

Y4 is a controlled physical-design closure experiment. It wraps Y3's verified RTL without altering the external contract or logical datapath, retains the 2.5 ns, 400 MHz constraint and 20% target utilization, and enables `CAP_MARGIN=20` during placement and post-global-route repair. The goal was to resolve Y3's 16 final extracted maximum-capacitance violations without conflating electrical repair with an RTL redesign.

The local Icarus regression passed K=7, K=0, and K=13 back-to-back operations, verifying all 768 output writes. The test deliberately changes external `k_dim` after launch, so this also confirms the latched-k control contract. Activation, weight, and output SRAMs remain external interface macros. Their 96 KiB total area is excluded from all reported standard-cell and core area figures.

The full Nangate45 ORFS flow completed on the four-core, 16 GiB GCS routing VM with `FLOW_RC=0`. Final extracted STA is fully clean: 451.475 MHz estimated fmax, 0.285036 ns setup slack, 0.00401651 ns hold slack, zero setup, hold, max-slew, max-fanout, and max-capacitance violations. The final extracted cap count is zero, which fixes Y3's 16 violations. Global routing passed at 435.17 MHz estimated fmax with 0.20 ns slack. Detailed routing repaired 31,736 initial violations to zero, with zero antenna net and pin violations.

Synthesis reports 176,092.532 um² of standard-cell area. The final route has 141,078 non-filler standard cells, 177,508 um² design area, 244,055 filler cells, and 3,686 tap cells inside an 878,559 um² core at 20.2045% utilization. Detailed routing took 2,835.66 seconds and peaked at 5,134.89 MB. Final routing uses 3,039,013 um of wire and 1,005,758 vias. The entire flow took 5,263 seconds from remote launch to terminal return. Power analysis estimates 254.894 mW total power, with 4.42162 mV worst VDD drop and 7.74259 mV worst VSS drop.

Relative to Y3, CAP_MARGIN=20 makes the design electrically clean but costs area and a little frequency: final routed area rose from 174,403 to 177,508 um² and final estimated fmax fell from 463.49 to 451.475 MHz. That is an acceptable cost for a clean extracted report. Y5 can now isolate the architecture question that Y2 and Y3 left open: replace the selected-bank shift drain with a local indexed readout, while retaining the same strict final-extraction checks.
